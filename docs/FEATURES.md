Percentage, age, average, ratio and other everyday calculations.

Mortgage, loan, car loan, EMI, compound interest, savings, retirement, ROI, profit margin, discount, tip, sales tax, down payment and related estimators.

## Health references
BMI, BMR, calorie needs, body fat, water intake, protein intake, running pace, sleep duration and related informational tools.

Unit price, invoice totals, manual currency conversion, fuel/trip cost, electricity cost, travel budget, square footage, paint, tile, concrete and recipe scaling.

Length, weight, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, data size, energy, power, pressure, angle, frequency and fuel economy.

Word count, character count, line count, sentence count, case conversion, whitespace cleanup, duplicate-line removal, line sorting, reverse text, find/replace, slug generation, URL encoding/decoding, Base64, HTML escaping and JSON/CSV utilities.

UUID, password, random number/string generation, SHA-256, JWT decode, Unix timestamp conversion, hexadecimal/binary conversion, regex testing, query parsing, user-agent viewing, screen-size viewing and IPv4 validation.

Keyword density, meta description checks, title length checks, UTM builder and robots.txt generation.

## Date & time
Date difference, business days, add/subtract days, week number, day of year, countdown, stopwatch, Pomodoro and world clock.

## Image tools
Image dimensions, resize, compression, PNG/JPEG/WebP conversion, rotation and grayscale processing.
